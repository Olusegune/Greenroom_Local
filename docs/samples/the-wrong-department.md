# THE WRONG DEPARTMENT
*A Greenroom Local Audio Performance Stress Test*

**SCENE 1 — VENOMSPUR'S OFFICE**

[SFX: A heavy iron door creaks open. Distant thunder. A clock ticks slowly. Papers shuffle. A faint furnace rumbles behind the walls.]

**MISS GRIMBLE:** (Clipped, professional) Next! Mr. Rico Blaze. Recording artist. Influencer. Self-proclaimed legend. Do come in.

**RICO:** (Confident, amused) Ah-ah! So this is the VIP lounge? Omo, this place serious o. Where's the champagne?

**VENOMSPUR:** (Without looking up; quietly) Champagne?

[PAUSE: 1 second]

**VENOMSPUR:** (Slowly removes his glasses; dry) My dear boy, this is an administrative office. Not an airport lounge.

**RICO:** (Laughs dismissively) Come on, boss. You know who I am? Twenty million followers. Three platinum records. I practically broke the internet.

**VENOMSPUR:** (Soft throat clearing) Yes. We noticed. Particularly the part where you sold your dignity for a sponsored cryptocurrency.

**RICO:** (Offended) That was a business partnership!

**VENOMSPUR:** (Pleasantly) Of course. And the Titanic was merely an ambitious swimming lesson.

[SFX: A distant demonic chuckle. One dry stamp hits paper.]

**MISS GRIMBLE:** (Matter-of-fact) Fraudulent endorsements, manufactured feuds, seventeen unpaid producers, and one extraordinarily unpleasant podcast.

**RICO:** (Defensive, fast) Wait, wait, wait. That podcast was satire! Everybody knows it's entertainment!

**VENOMSPUR:** (Very soft, deliberate) How fascinating that everything becomes satire once the consequences arrive.

[PAUSE: 2 seconds]

**RICO:** (More cautious) Okay... so where exactly am I?

**VENOMSPUR:** (Cheerfully) Ah. The question of the hour.

[SFX: Deep furnace rumble grows slightly louder.]

**VENOMSPUR:** (Whispers, close to microphone) You're in Hell, Mr. Blaze.

**RICO:** (Nervous laugh, then silence) Ha! Nice one. Good prank. Where are the cameras?

**MISS GRIMBLE:** (Flat) We discontinued cameras after the incident with the influencers.

**RICO:** (Quietly) Which incident?

**MISS GRIMBLE:** (Sighs) All of them.

---

**SCENE 2 — AN UNEXPECTED INTERRUPTION**

[SFX: Rapid footsteps. Door bursts open. Keys rattle.]

**MAMA T:** (Furious, projecting) Ẹ̀yin people! Who is in charge of this nonsense?!

**RICO:** (Startled) Mama T?!

**MAMA T:** (Astonished, scolding) Rico?! You?! Even here you are causing trouble?!

**VENOMSPUR:** (Irritated throat clearing) Madam, this is a private disciplinary proceeding.

**MAMA T:** (Sarcastically) Private? With this noise? I heard his foolishness from three corridors away!

**RICO:** (Embarrassed whisper) Mama, please. You're ruining my image.

**MAMA T:** (Short explosive laugh) Image?! My son, your image has already expired!

[SFX: Papers fall. Miss Grimble suppresses a laugh.]

**VENOMSPUR:** (Trying to maintain control) If we might return to the matter at hand—

**MAMA T:** (Interrupting sharply) Excuse me! Are you the manager?

**VENOMSPUR:** (A beat, dignified) I am the Senior Undersecretary of Eternal Admissions.

**MAMA T:** (Unimpressed) So you're not the manager.

[PAUSE: 2 seconds]

**MISS GRIMBLE:** (Barely suppressing laughter) Technically, sir, she's correct.

**VENOMSPUR:** (Long irritated exhale) Grimble. Not. Another. Word.

**RICO:** (Hopeful) Mama T, you came to rescue me?

**MAMA T:** (Tender for a moment) Ah, my child...

[PAUSE: 1 second]

**MAMA T:** (Suddenly furious) I came to collect the money you borrowed!

**RICO:** (Panicking) Even here?!

**MAMA T:** (Matter-of-fact) Debt does not disappear because you changed location!

[SFX: One loud thunderclap. Distant demonic laughter.]

**VENOMSPUR:** (Exhausted, muttering) I should have retired during the Middle Ages.

---

**SCENE 3 — THE FINAL VERDICT**

[SFX: Furnace settles. Clock ticks. Venomspur straightens a stack of papers.]

**VENOMSPUR:** (Controlled, icy politeness) Mr. Blaze. Your appeal has been reviewed.

**RICO:** (Softly, hopeful) And?

**VENOMSPUR:** (Long pause; serene) The committee has reached its decision.

[PAUSE: 2 seconds]

**RICO:** (Barely audible whisper) Please...

**VENOMSPUR:** (Sudden cheerful brightness) Congratulations!

**RICO:** (Shouting joyfully) YES! I knew it!

**VENOMSPUR:** (Casually) You've been appointed Assistant Deputy Coordinator of Unanswered Customer Complaints.

**RICO:** (Confused) Wait... what?

**MISS GRIMBLE:** (Matter-of-fact, fast) Six thousand complaints per hour. No lunch breaks. No weekends. No mute button.

**RICO:** (Horrified) No mute button?!

**VENOMSPUR:** (With immense satisfaction) And every complaint is delivered as a seventeen-minute voice note.

[SFX: A notification chime. Then another. Then rapid overlapping notification chimes.]

**RICO:** (Genuine terror) NOOOOO!

**MAMA T:** (Satisfied, laughing) Good! Maybe now you will learn to listen!

**VENOMSPUR:** (Exhausted sigh, then quietly) Grimble?

**MISS GRIMBLE:** Yes, sir?

**VENOMSPUR:** (Dryly) Put in my request for annual leave.

**MISS GRIMBLE:** (Brightly) Certainly, sir. Which millennium?

[PAUSE: 2 seconds]

**VENOMSPUR:** (Defeated whisper) Oh, good heavens.

[SFX: The office clock strikes thirteen. A final heavy stamp. Fade to silence.]

**END**
