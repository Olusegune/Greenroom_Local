# Commercial use and licensing

Greenroom is software. Selling or using it commercially does **not** make anything it generates commercially licensed. Each voice and each engine has its own terms, and the owner is responsible for checking them. This page states what Greenroom does and does not promise.

## What Greenroom shows and enforces

| Situation | What Greenroom does |
|---|---|
| Voice licence | Each voice has a licence field: unknown, commercial or personal, with a note and the date it was checked. Unknown is the default and is shown as unknown. |
| Cloned voices | Cloning needs a recorded permission flag and consent details (who agreed, on what basis, a reference, a date). A cloned voice with no consent record is refused by the Agent Studio, flagged by the audio check, and warned about when a preset is applied. |
| Presets | A preset is written text. Its licence says so and says that rights to a voice belong to that voice's own engine and licence. It never states or implies commercial rights. |
| Local models | WazobiaVoice is a research licence, non-commercial. Other local engines show their licence in Model Center. No model weights are bundled with Greenroom or with the presets. |
| Cloud engines | Paid. Every paid call goes through the cost sheet and the ledger first; the Agent Studio also enforces a spending limit and never retries a paid step by itself. |
| Local-only projects | The Agent Studio sends nothing to a cloud engine when local-only is on (the default). |
| Accents | Starter presets are labelled unverified or experimental. Nothing claims accent authenticity. |

## Five categories to keep apart

1. **Free local use**: runs on this PC, no per-use charge. Still subject to the model's licence.
2. **Commercially permitted use**: only where the voice or model's licence says so and the voice is marked commercial.
3. **Non-commercial use**: for example research licences. Do not sell the output.
4. **Restricted or unknown rights**: treat as not cleared until you have checked.
5. **Paid cloud generation**: billed by the provider; commercial rights depend on the provider's plan and terms.

## Not verified by Greenroom

Provider terms change. Greenroom has not had any provider licence reviewed by a lawyer, and does not offer legal advice. The Portrait library's rights were recorded as owner-supplied on the owner's statement.
