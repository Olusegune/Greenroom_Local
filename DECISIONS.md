| D-22 | 2026-10-26 | Timeline: lines are moved by setting their cue, so the stored script stays plain. | D-23 | 2026-10-26 | Writing a video with the mix uses ffmpeg as a separate program, never re-encoding the picture (stream copy; the original is never changed).
